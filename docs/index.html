Privacy and data

The garden grows.
Your data stays on your computer.

Pixel Agent Garden reads local agent data only. It never uploads activity, collects telemetry, or modifies Claude and Codex source files.

Nothing is uploaded Scanning and rendering happen entirely on this computer.
No behavior tracking No analytics, remote crash reports, hidden usage events, or remote logs.
Agent files stay untouched Claude, Codex, and future source directories are always treated as read-only input.
Local state stays in your control Cache, settings, and pricing data live under ~/.local-agent-garden/ for you to inspect or remove.
